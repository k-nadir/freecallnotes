Before you record

Get consent to record
In many countries and US states, everyone on a call must agree to being recorded. Tell the other people on the call and get their consent before you press Record. You are responsible for following the law where you and they are, and your organisation's rules.
Transcripts can be wrong
Transcripts are made automatically by speech recognition and can contain mistakes, especially names and numbers. Check anything important against the recording before you rely on it.
Take care with AI assistants
The "Transcript for AI" file contains everything said on the call. Only give it to an AI assistant you and your organisation are allowed to use, because that service will receive the content.
Your recordings stay here
Recordings and transcripts are saved only on this computer. FreeCallNotes connects to the internet only to download its speech models and to check for updates (at most every 30 days, or when you ask). Updates are only installed if you choose to.
Call pop-ups
When a call app or your browser starts using the microphone, FreeCallNotes can show a small pop-up offering to open it. A tiny helper starts with Windows for this; it never records. Turn it off in Options, or click "Don't show again" on the pop-up.
