A free, offline alternative to cloud AI notetakers

Cloud notetakers such as Otter.ai and Fireflies send your call audio to their servers to transcribe it. FreeCallNotes does the transcription on your own Windows PC instead, for free. Here's what that gets you, what it doesn't do, and how to decide.

What you get

  • Free and open source. No subscription, no account, no minutes limit. The code is public under GPL-3.0, so anyone can check what it does.
  • Private by design. Recordings and transcripts stay on your computer. The app has no analytics and uploads nothing.
  • No bot in your meetings. It records the sound on your PC, so nobody has to admit a notetaker.
  • No installation. Unzip and double-click. No admin rights needed.
  • Knows who said what. Your voice and the other side are recorded as separate tracks.
  • Works with any call app. Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, Webex, Slack huddles, WhatsApp desktop, or a call in a browser.
  • Notes in one step. The "Transcript for AI" file gives any AI assistant the transcript and the instructions together.

What to know before you switch

  • It's Windows only (10 and 11, 64-bit) and English only for now.
  • It writes the transcript; you use your own AI assistant for the summary.
  • It separates you from the other side, but doesn't yet tell the other people apart.
  • It only records calls on the PC it runs on, not calls on your phone.
  • The first run downloads a speech model of about 660 MB. After that it works offline.
  • Transcripts are accurate enough for great notes, but check names and numbers.

Who it suits

  • People who don't want call audio on a third-party server: client calls, interviews, sensitive conversations.
  • Anyone whose meetings don't allow notetaker bots, or who'd rather not ask.
  • Freelancers and small teams who don't want another subscription.
  • People who already use an AI assistant and just need a good transcript to give it.

How to start

  1. Download FreeCallNotes and unzip it.
  2. Double-click FreeCallNotes.exe and accept the short "Before you record" notice. The first time, wait for the speech model to download.
  3. Tell people you're recording, press Record when your call starts, and Stop when it ends.
  4. Click "Show transcript for AI" and drop the file into your AI assistant for notes.

Questions

Is FreeCallNotes affiliated with Otter.ai or Fireflies?

No. FreeCallNotes is an independent open-source project. Product names are trademarks of their owners and are used only to describe what FreeCallNotes is an alternative to.

Can I import my old recordings?

Not yet. FreeCallNotes transcribes calls it records itself.

Can my company use it?

Yes, it's free for work use. Follow your employer's policies on recording and AI tools.

Where are my recordings saved?

In the Call Notes folder next to the app, one dated folder per call. You can change the location in Options.

Does it need a powerful PC?

No GPU is needed. 8 GB of RAM is recommended. On slower PCs you can transcribe after the call instead of live.
