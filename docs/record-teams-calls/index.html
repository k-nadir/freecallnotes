How to record and transcribe Microsoft Teams calls on Windows

FreeCallNotes records your voice and the other people on a Teams call as two separate tracks, then transcribes them on your own PC. It's free, open source and needs no installation.

Record a Teams call in five steps

  1. Download FreeCallNotes and unzip it anywhere, such as your Documents folder.
  2. Double-click FreeCallNotes.exe. The first time, accept the short "Before you record" notice and wait while the speech model downloads (about 660 MB, once).
  3. Join your meeting in the Teams desktop app or in your browser.
  4. Tell everyone you're recording and get their agreement, then press Record.
  5. Press Stop when the call ends. The transcript is ready within moments, in a dated folder with both audio tracks.

Why it works with Teams

FreeCallNotes doesn't connect to Teams at all. It records your default microphone and whatever plays through your default speakers or headphones, the same audio you hear. That means it works in the Teams app, Teams in a browser, and any other call app, and it doesn't stop Teams from using your microphone.

Tips for a clean Teams transcript

  • Wear headphones, so your mic doesn't pick up the other side from your speakers.
  • Make sure the headset you use in Teams is also your Windows default microphone and speaker.
  • Check names and numbers in the transcript before you share notes.

Turn the transcript into meeting notes

Click "Show transcript for AI" and drop the file into the AI assistant you're allowed to use. The file contains the transcript and the instructions together, so you get a summary, decisions and action items in one step.

Recording at work

FreeCallNotes needs no installation or admin rights, but you still need to follow your employer's policies on recording calls and using AI tools, and the law where you and the other people are. Always get consent before you record.

Questions

Does the other side know I'm recording?

FreeCallNotes doesn't announce itself in Teams, so you need to tell people yourself and get their agreement before you record.

Do I need a Teams licence or admin permission?

No. FreeCallNotes records the audio on your own PC, so it doesn't depend on Teams recording features or on admin rights.

Is it free?

Yes. It's free and open source under GPL-3.0, with no account and no limits.
