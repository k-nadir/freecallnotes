Transcribe Zoom calls without a bot joining the meeting

Most AI notetakers join your Zoom meeting as an extra participant. FreeCallNotes doesn't join anything: it runs on your Windows PC, records both sides of the call and transcribes it locally.

How it works

FreeCallNotes listens to your microphone and your computer's sound, the same audio that reaches your headphones. Nobody sees a notetaker in the participant list, and your audio never leaves your computer.

  1. Download and unzip FreeCallNotes, then double-click FreeCallNotes.exe. There's nothing to install.
  2. Start your Zoom call in the Zoom app or in your browser.
  3. Tell people you're recording and get their agreement, then press Record.
  4. Press Stop at the end. You get a transcript plus two audio tracks: you, and the other side.
  5. Drop the "Transcript for AI" file into your AI assistant for a summary, decisions and action items.

Bot notetaker or local recorder?

  • No extra participant. The meeting looks exactly as it would without FreeCallNotes.
  • No upload. Speech recognition runs on your PC with NVIDIA Nemotron, and optionally Moonshine.
  • Works offline after the first model download.
  • Any app. The same tool works for Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, Webex and calls in a browser.

Not having a bot doesn't remove the need for consent. Always tell people you're recording and follow the law where you and they are.

Questions

Does Zoom need to allow recording?

FreeCallNotes records the sound on your own PC rather than using Zoom's recording feature, but you still need everyone's consent.

Does it work on Mac?

Not yet. FreeCallNotes is Windows-only for now.

What languages does it transcribe?

English only, for now.
