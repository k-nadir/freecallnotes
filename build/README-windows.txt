FREECALLNOTES
Free, private call recording and transcription for Windows.

START
Double-click "FreeCallNotes" (FreeCallNotes.exe). After the first run there is also a
FreeCallNotes icon on your desktop. Nothing needs to be installed.

The first time, FreeCallNotes downloads its speech model (about 660 MB) into the "models"
folder. After that it works without the internet.

RECORD A CALL
1. Start your call in Zoom, Teams or any other app.
2. Tell the other people you are recording and get their agreement.
3. Press Record in FreeCallNotes. Press Stop when the call ends.
4. Your call is saved in the "Call Notes" folder (change it in Options).
   Drop the "Transcript for AI" file into your AI assistant to get clean notes.

REMOVE
Turn off "Call pop-ups" in Options (this removes its Startup shortcut), close FreeCallNotes
and delete this folder (and the desktop icon).
